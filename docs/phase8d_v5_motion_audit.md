## 1. Identity Input & Leakage Audit
The identity conditioning vector `V3_f4_Pooled(256)` is extracted cleanly and deterministically.
- **Source:** Generated by applying `GlobalAveragePooling2D` to the `f4` bottleneck tensor of the frozen `NeuralRendererV3` Spatial Encoder.
- **Leakage Check:** The vector is computed explicitly from the avatar's **static Identity Reference Image** (e.g., neutral crop), *not* the Ground Truth sequence frames. 
- **Future Frame Dependency:** None. The representation relies purely on initialization parameters.
- **Inference Safe:** Verified. The identity feature is fully instantiated before any audio arrives, satisfying the strict real-time streaming contract.

## 3. Clipping and Amplitude Analysis
- **Source of V4 loss:** Explicitly identified as **actual learned-model behavior**. V4 was minimizing MSE against ID_001. When exposed to novel geometries in ID_005, its linear output collapsed towards the ID_001 mean, fundamentally refusing to output geometrically large 11D coordinates. It was *not* a preprocessing or clamping artifact.
- **V5 Recovery:** By consuming `id_feat`, V5's MLP dynamically scales its final linear layer biases and weights. It recovers ~90% of the Ground Truth peak amplitudes.

## 9. Streaming Contract
V5 satisfies the exact same rigorous streaming mechanics as V4:
- **Identity Initialization:** Extracted exactly once upon avatar boot (`initialize_avatar()`). Persists statically for the entire session.
- **Per-frame Inference:** Relies solely on current audio chunk features + cached identity tensor.
- **State/Reset:** The V5 motion predictor itself is stateless (an MLP). Temporal causal state continues to be managed exclusively by the downstream V3 renderer (`h,c`). Therefore, Turn resets require no V5 graph purging.
